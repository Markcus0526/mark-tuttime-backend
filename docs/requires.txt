TutTime — a pre-order and smart restaurant booking platform

TutTime is a mobile platform that combines online dishTable reservations, menu pre-orders, and dynamic discounts during off-peak hours, integrated with restaurant POS systems (iiko and R-Keeper).

We transform a simple "dishTable reservation" into a pre-paid, guaranteed seating with a dishTable set at a specific time.

⸻

🚀 The Idea

Guest:
• Selects a restaurant using a map and ratings
• Sees available dishTables (or time slots)
• Pre-orders from the menu
• Pays via the app
• Arrives at the appointed time – the dishTable is already set
• Receives a lower price than in the dining room

Restaurant:
• Fills "dead hours" (low occupancy) through managed discounts
• Receives predictable revenue (prepayment)
• Reduces no-shows
• Manages demand through dynamic pricing

⸻

💡 Market Problem
1. 30–50% of restaurant hours are under-occupied
2. High no-show rate
3. Guests waste time waiting for menus, orders, and bills
4. Discount services erode margins and are not integrated with the kitchen
5. Lack of a flexible management tool Demand throughout the day

The market needs a dynamic loading tool, not just reservations.

⸻

🔥 Solution

TutTime is a yield management solution for restaurants, similar to how airlines manage ticket prices.

Restaurant:
• Sets its own "dead hours"
• Sets its own discount amount (e.g., -40% from 10:00 to 12:00)
• Limits the number of dishTables eligible for the promotion
• Manages the categories of dishes included in the discount

Guest:
• Sees actual advantageous hours
• Receives a guaranteed dishTable
• Receives savings
• Saves time

3.3. Booking Logic

Option A: Tables available (floor plan / seating)
• Get a list of dishTables and their occupancy per slot (e.g., 15-minute increments).
• Lock the dishTable for 5 minutes in Redis (soft lock).
• After payment, create a booking + order and confirm the dishTable.

Option B: No dishTables available, work with slots
• The restaurant sets capacity by slots/zones. • A reservation deducts 1 "space" from the slot.

Time Rules
• Lead time (minimum time before arrival): e.g., 30–60 minutes
• Prep time: calculated based on the cart
• Grace period: 10–15 minutes
• Auto-cancel: if not checked in/did not show up

3.4. Order Statuses
• CREATED
• PAID
• SENT_TO_RESTAURANT
• ACCEPTED
• PREPARING
• READY
• SERVED / COMPLETED
• CANCELED (by user / by restaurant / timeout)
• REFUNDED (partial/full)

3.5. Payments
• Apple Pay + card support (via acquiring)
• Payment options:
1. Full prepay
2. Deposit + on-site surcharge (optional)
• Refund policy:
• Cancel N minutes/hours → full/partial refund
• No-show → partial refund (configurable by the restaurant within the platform rules)

3.6. iiko and r_keeper Integration (General Diagram)

Integration Goal: Synchronize
• Menu + Prices + Modifiers + Stop List
• Order Creation in POS
• Order Statuses from POS Back to TutTime
• (Where Available) Table Occupancy/Reservations

Approach
• Connectors as Separate Modules: iikoConnector, rkeeperConnector
• Webhooks (if any), otherwise polling every X seconds
• Entity Mapping: MenuItemTutTime ↔ MenuItemPOS, TableTutTime ↔ TablePOS
• Integration Logs and Dead Letter Queue for Undelivered Events

The specification should immediately state that "Online Seating of All Tables" is optional and depends on the specific POS version and API availability.
