# Internal Code of Conduct (Team)

## Our Commitment

As a member of the TutTime development team, you agree to:

- Respect intellectual property – never share code, credentials, or architecture details outside the company.
- Keep all repositories, documents, and discussions private.
- Report any observed security issue immediately to the lead engineer.
- Use professional and respectful language in all internal communication (chat, email, code reviews).

## Enforcement

Violations of this code of conduct may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of access and employment.

**This is an internal document – do not distribute externally.**
